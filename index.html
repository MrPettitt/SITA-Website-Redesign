Who We Are

The ICT Engine Behind South African Government

Established in 1999, SITA consolidates and coordinates the State's information technology resources, delivering scale, capability and interoperability across all spheres of government.

As the IT business partner to the largest employer and consumer of IT products in South Africa, SITA ensures every department gets value for money while keeping citizen services secure and accessible.

Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use SITA's services?

SITA serves national and provincial government departments and other public-sector entities. Some services are mandatory for departments, while others are optional and available on request.

How do I log a service request?

Use the IT Service Management portal, call the toll-free helpline on 0800 748 222, or send us a message with the form below and choose "Service Request".

How can my company become a SITA supplier?

Review the tender and RFQ bulletins in the Procurement Hub, then register as a supplier through the e-procurement portal. Use the contact form with the "Procurement" enquiry type if you need help.

When is the contact centre open?

The head office contact centre operates Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:30. You can email contact.centre@sita.co.za at any time.
